## Direction contract

THESIS: The live stage shows one object at a time at gallery scale — no buttons, no chrome, nothing to click. The category default for an auction/bidding UI (a toolbar of icon buttons: +, -, confirm) is refused entirely; the room's MC controls it with a keyboard alone, silently, so the audience only ever sees the object and the number.

OWN-WORLD: True near-black ground (not navy, not warm-brown — a hard reset from the previous "Gilded Gavel" world). One warm gold accent, restrained to price, one hairline divider, the item-counter badge, and the strike moment — never a wash, never boxed. Bold, flat gold numerals with no per-digit frame or border (the previous world's coin-tile digits are retired). Display voice is a modern geometric grotesk (Bahnschrift, latin/numeric only) for price and labels; Thai item copy stays in the existing Sarabun-safe sans since Bahnschrift has no Thai glyphs. A large rounded-square product plate (28px radius) carries a floating dark-glass item-counter badge on its corner. Unit is "Token", not currency.

STORY: The MC, and by extension the room watching the projector, reads three facts at a glance: this is the item, this is its current bid in Tokens, and the room is one keystroke from a decision. Nothing on screen invites a click because nothing can be clicked — nav row, bid buttons, and the confirm modal's buttons are all retired in favor of a silent keyboard legend.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed 16:9 dark stage, no visible top nav during live mode. Left ~45%: rounded-square product plate at generous scale, item-counter badge ("1 / 4") floating on its top-right corner. Right ~55%: item name in the Thai-safe sans (large), a single thin gold hairline divider, a small-caps "TOKEN ปัจจุบัน" label, then the huge bold flat gold price + "Token" unit carrying the digit-roll signature motion (mechanic kept from the previous world, restyled flat/unframed). A quiet keyboard legend is anchored to the bottom edge (← → navigate · ↑ ↓ bid · Enter strike), togglable with H. On strike (Enter), the gavel motion fires once, then an inline borderless text field appears in place of the bid line for the winner's name — typed and confirmed with Enter, cancelled with Escape — never a modal with clickable buttons.

FORM: Built directly from the user's own pinned reference image (a dark-luxury antiquities/jewelry auction app: near-black ground, rounded-square gold-bordered product plate, floating "20 Bids" glass badge, bold gold price, clean geometric sans) plus their explicit interaction brief (no buttons, arrow-key navigation, ±1 Token bidding, Enter to strike). Per new-work.md, "a user- or brief-pinned decision beats the roll, always": `impeccable concept-seed --scope direction --mode experience` was run for traceability (seed key 9a9d5a2c, assigned index 4 against an unauthored candidate list) and its six dealt challengers were weighed — the nixie-tube laboratory-counter challenger (SOURCE ID signals-instruments-nixie-laboratory-counter) donated one confirming discipline already present in the kept odometer mechanic ("a count change is a visible physical event, not a swap"); the remaining five (blacksmith forging, drawcord cape, itasha vinyl wrap, variety-show telop captions, ASCII live-render) were declined as ungrounded in an antiquities/jewelry-auction subject. No candidate outranked the user's own pinned reference, which is the binding direction.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
